This article documents the Smart Fab Edge AI system architecture I presented in an interview presentation,
expanding the original 11-slide deck into a more comprehensive technical reference.
The project metrics cited in each section are collected in the appendix at the end,
together with their measurement caveats.
Starting with equipment sensor data, the article walks through real-time data processing,
anomaly detection, Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction, edge deployment,
industrial knowledge retrieval, and the decision boundaries of AI agents.
The question it actually tries to answer is not simply how to deploy AI models in a factory,
but how to keep them running under constrained resources —
trading off latency, compute, reliability, and operating cost —
while integrating with existing equipment controls, maintenance workflows, and factory information systems.
